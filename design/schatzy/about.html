HOUSE RULES

01

ORDER AT THE COUNTER

No table service, no QR code, no app. Say it out loud to a person.

02

DON'T MAKE A MESS

The sauce bar is a privilege. Wipe the drip, stack your boat, we all move on.

03

STOOLS ARE FOR EATING

Twelve seats for a whole neighbourhood. Laptops after 3pm only, and don't push it.

04

CASH IS FINE

Card is fine too. Tips go in the tin and get split on Sunday — it's real money to us.

05

WE CLOSE WHEN WE SELL OUT

Some Fridays that means 10pm. Follow us if you hate disappointment.

06

BE NICE TO THE CREW

They are your cool cousins. Cousins get respect.

ONE GRIDDLE,
TWO CITIES

Half the recipes come from a grandmother in Mapo-gu, half from a Späti fridge. Kimchi and pulled pork sit on the same counter and nobody has complained yet. We change two fillings a week to keep ourselves honest.
